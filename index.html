It's all about perspective

Introducing Yourself Is Weird.

You want to show your best work without sounding like you're showing off. You want to be honest about the things you're still figuring out without accidentally talking yourself down. You want people to believe you're capable, while secretly wondering whether you're making the right decisions yourself.

Those thoughts live in my head more often than I'd like to admit.

"Luckily, I've learned that most of those questions aren't answered by overthinking them—they're answered by doing. Building things. Meeting people. Trying something that sounds way too ambitious, and somehow figuring it out along the way."

That's pretty much how I've approached my entire career so far.

Origins & Curiosity

SO... WHO IS JOEY?

I'm Joey van der Linden, a Dutch multimedia designer with an unhealthy curiosity for anything involving cameras, computers, design, or technology.

As a kid I wasn't the type who had one dream job. One week I wanted to become a filmmaker. The next week I was fascinated by websites. Then 3D graphics. Then programming. Then photography. Then drones. Instead of choosing one, I decided to chase all of them.

Growing up during a time where technology evolved almost every year probably didn't help. Every new tool opened another rabbit hole, and naturally I wanted to jump into every single one of them.

October 26, 2023 Milestone

Received my propaedeutic certificate—the moment I realized I wasn't just "interested" in creative technology, but wanted to build a future around it.

Capabilities & Skillset

WHAT CAN I ACTUALLY DO?

Short answer? A little bit of everything.

Long answer? I've spent the last few years collecting creative skills like they're Pokémon. Photography. Filmmaking. Motion graphics. Graphic design. UX/UI. Project management. 3D scanning. Web development. Virtual Reality. Storytelling.

I never liked putting myself into a single box. Every project teaches something different, and surprisingly, all those different disciplines make each other stronger. Understanding design helps my filmmaking. Filmmaking improves my storytelling. Storytelling makes better websites. Everything connects.

July 27, 2022 • The Spark

Bought my first DSLR camera—a Canon 7D. Looking back, buying that camera might have been one of the most expensive hobbies I've ever started... because it quickly turned into an obsession. Since then I've spent countless hours chasing good light, waiting for the perfect moment, editing until 2 AM, and occasionally laying on the ground just to get "the shot." Totally worth it.

Purpose & Vision

WHAT DRIVES ME?

I care about making things that matter. Sure, creating something that looks beautiful is satisfying. But creating something that changes how people think, feel, or behave—that's the exciting part.

Whether that's a documentary that gives someone a new perspective, an interactive experience that teaches people something without them realizing it, or a commercial that people actually remember instead of skipping... That's the kind of work I want to make.

The Dream

"To build media that leaves a lasting impression long after the screen turns black."

The Future

I'm still figuring things out. Honestly, I hope I never completely stop. Every project introduces me to new people, new ideas, and new opportunities I couldn't have predicted beforehand. That's exactly what makes this industry so exciting.
